# Application surfaces

Mode: Operate; Read for learning documents and guidance.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Course catalog makes daily study, curriculum exploration, and review distinct jobs. Remove the promotional roadmap hero and decorative metric-card composition.

OWN-WORLD: Ink-blue navigation, warm off-white ground, white reading surfaces, forest-green actions, and explicit semantic states. One self-hosted sans family, disciplined spacing, compact ruled lists, and conventional controls.

STORY: The learner sees the current or next ready task, its outcome and deadline, then starts or resumes work. Visitors can browse the complete curriculum. Secondary information expands in place.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Grouped sidebar at left, concise header above the work area. A wide current-task section leads with an action and timer, with deadlines and study time beside it. Next tasks follow as aligned records. The signature is consistent task ID, outcome, schedule, and evidence alignment across reading and working states. Mobile stacks these sections without shrinking text.

FORM: University course catalog, grounded candidate 6 from seed a3e91749; explicitly chosen by the user after switching from Emission rail. Code-first. Keep expressive theme reveal and reduced-motion fallback; no decorative page entrances.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
