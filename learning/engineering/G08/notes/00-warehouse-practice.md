# G08 — Rehearse deployment failure and recovery

Week 16 · Core · Warehouse checkpoint B16

## Review and learn

Deployment rollback, database compatibility, smoke checks and restoration.

## Do the work

Deploy a deliberately bad change in staging or a local simulation. Restore service and verify old data.

## Pass check

Record time to recover, smoke output and the restore or rollback steps.

Write the implementation and checks yourself. Use documentation, the debugger and actual runs. Save what you ran, what happened, and your next action in [Results](../results/evidence.md) or record it in Learnspace. Include a commit or result path when useful.
